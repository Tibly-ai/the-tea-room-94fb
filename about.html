The Origin of Our Space

Crafted out of a quiet need for patience.

The Tea Room was founded in 2018 in a historic warehouse room. Our vision was simple: to create a tactile shelter from the modern noise, dedicated entirely to the focused preparation of organic loose-leaf teas.

We began with six single-origin teas sourced from traditional family estates. Today, we remain small by design, choosing to nurture deep connections with those who tend the soil rather than scaling beyond our personal standards of hospitality.

Our Core Philosophy

Grounded in reciprocity, brewed with intention.

01

Direct-Trade Micro-Farms

We bypass industrial auction houses entirely. By working directly with multi-generational families in Uji, Darjeeling, and Yunnan, we guarantee above-market rates directly to the farmers who maintain original heirloom cultivars.

Traceable origins
02

Biodynamic Cultivation

Every estate we partner with relies on natural biodiversity rather than chemical fertilizers. Soil composition, mountain spring runoff, and natural shade cover define the true seasonal vintage of each harvest.

Organic farming standard
03

Zero-Waste Brewing

Water temperature is metered precisely to avoid redundant energy loss. Used tea leaves are dried, packaged, and provided to neighborhood gardeners for composting, returning essential nutrients back to the earth.

Circular waste system
