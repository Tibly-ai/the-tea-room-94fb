Our Philosophy

We treat the preparation of tea not as a transaction, but as a meditation.

Opened Spring 2019 128 Sage Way, Suite A, Portland, Oregon 97201

Every leaf in The Tea Room tells a quiet story of geography, weather, and patient hands. We travel directly to family estates in Uji, Darjeeling, and Fujian to select harvests that prioritize soil health and traditional processing over industrial yields.

Our space is arranged to honor your stillness. You will find no loud espresso grinds, no blaring background speakers, and no fast-paced lines. Instead, water is boiled in heavy cast iron tetsubin kettle styles, and tea is steeped at your table using simple clay vessels suited perfectly to each leaf's natural character.

Our Ritual

The quiet steps to beautiful extraction

01

Sourcing Leaf Integrity

We source solely whole-leaf teas harvested by growers who prioritize natural biodiversity. No additives, flavorings, or hidden oils are ever introduced.

02

Perfect Spring Water

Water quality makes the steep. We filter our soft spring water to an exact, mineral-rich balance that carries delicate leaf compounds beautifully.

03

Vessel Calibration

Porous Yixing clay for earthy pu-erh, hand-thrown porcelain for light-floral green teas. Every set is matched to its unique physical properties.

04

Sustained Presence

We step back and allow you to appreciate multiple infusions in peace. Enjoy the slow rhythm of expanding leaf aroma at your own natural speed.
