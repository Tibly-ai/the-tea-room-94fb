House Etiquette

A quiet space to rest your thoughts.

We curate our environment specifically to support deep relaxation and creative work. To preserve the peaceful nature of The Tea Room, we kindly ask all guests to observe a few simple practices during their visit.

01

Gently hushed voices

We maintain low conversation volumes so everyone can read, write, or sit in silent reflection undisturbed.

02

Digital mindfulness

Laptops and writing are always welcome, but we ask that phone calls be taken outside and all devices remain silent.

03

Patience in preparation

Our teas are brewed with exact water temperatures and timed steeps. Each service is custom served at the pace of your breath.
