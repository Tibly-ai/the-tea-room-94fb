The Philosophy

Brewed for contemplation, never rushed.

Our offerings are simple by design. We choose to highlight single-origin teas that tell a story of their respective landscapes, rainfall, and traditional preparation methods.

We do not offer artificial flavors, heavily sweetened syrups, or rushed preparation. Every serving of tea is provided in a clay or porcelain vessel suited to its optimal leaf expansion, allowing you to watch the leaves unfold as your mind settles.

Our pastries are baked daily in small quantities, utilizing local organic grains, unsalted sweet butter, and subtle floral infusions to complement rather than overpower the delicate flavor profiles of our tea selection.
